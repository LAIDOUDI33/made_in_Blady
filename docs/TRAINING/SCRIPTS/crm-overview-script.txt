--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

NARRATOR:
"Chapter 1: The Dashboard Overview.

When you first log into the CRM, you'll see your personalized dashboard. 
This is your command center - everything you need at a glance."

[ACTION: Click on "Total Contacts" KPI card]

NARRATOR:
"At the top, you'll see six key performance indicator cards:

First, Total Contacts - this shows everyone in your database. As you can see, 
I have 1,247 contacts. This number updates in real-time as you add new ones."

[ACTION: Move to "Active Deals" card]

NARRATOR:
"Active Deals shows how many opportunities you're currently working on. 
Right now, I have 23 active deals in various stages of the sales process."

[ACTION: Move to "Pipeline Value" card]

NARRATOR:
"Pipeline Value is one of the most important metrics - it shows the total 
value of all your active opportunities. Here we see 4.5 million DZD in 
potential revenue."

[ACTION: Move to remaining cards - Win Rate, Tasks Due, New Leads]

NARRATOR:
"Your Win Rate tracks your closing percentage. Aim for at least 25%.

Tasks Due Today keeps you organized - I can see I have 5 tasks that need 
attention.

And New Leads shows how many prospects were added in the past week - great 
for tracking marketing effectiveness."

VISUAL: Show navigation bar and highlight each item

NARRATOR:
"Now let's look at the main navigation. You have five main sections:

Dashboard - where we are now
Contacts - your complete database
Deals - your sales pipeline
Analytics - reports and insights
Settings - preferences and configuration"

[ACTION: Show Quick Actions bar]

NARRATOR:
"Below the navigation, you'll find the Quick Actions Bar. These are your 
one-click shortcuts for common tasks. Let me demonstrate..."

[ACTION: Click "+ New Contact"]

NARRATOR:
"Clicking 'New Contact' opens the contact creation form instantly. We'll 
explore this in detail in Chapter 2."

[ACTION: Close modal, hover over other quick actions]

NARRATOR:
"You also have shortcuts for creating deals, logging activities, importing 
contacts, and adding tasks. These save you valuable time throughout the day."

[SECTION 2: CONTACT MANAGEMENT - 3:30 to 7:00]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

VISUAL: Navigate to Contacts page, show list view

NARRATOR:
"Chapter 2: Contact Management.

Your contacts are the heart of your CRM. Let's explore how to add, organize, 
and segment them effectively."

[ACTION: Click "+ New Contact", show empty form]

NARRATOR:
"To add a new contact manually, click the 'New Contact' button. You'll see 
this form with several fields."

[ACTION: Fill in sample data - Ahmed Benali from TechAlgeria]

NARRATOR:
"The required fields are marked with an asterisk: First Name, Last Name, 
and Email Address. Email is important because it serves as the unique 
identifier for each contact."

[ACTION: Fill in optional fields - phone, company, job title]

NARRATOR:
"I highly recommend filling in Phone Number, Company, and Job Title when 
available. This information helps with personalization and segmentation later."

[ACTION: Scroll down to Tags field, add tags]

NARRATOR:
"Tags are powerful organizational tools. I'm going to add 'prospect', 'b2b', 
and 'algiers' to categorize this contact. We'll talk more about tags in a moment."

[ACTION: Click Save Contact, show success message]

NARRATOR:
"Clicking Save adds the contact to your database. Simple as that!"

VISUAL: Navigate to Contacts → Import

NARRATOR:
"But what if you have hundreds of contacts to add? That's where bulk import 
comes in handy."

[ACTION: Show import page, highlight Download Template button]

NARRATOR:
"First, download our CSV template. This ensures your data format matches 
our system requirements exactly."

[ACTION: Show template in Excel/Sheets]

NARRATOR:
"The template includes columns for name, email, phone, company, and more. 
Fill in your data, save as CSV, and you're ready to upload."

[ACTION: Show file upload, mapping preview]

NARRATOR:
"When you upload your file, we'll show you a preview and let you map your 
columns to our database fields. Review carefully, then click Import."

[ACTION: Show success message with summary]

NARRATOR:
"In just moments, hundreds of contacts are added! The system automatically 
skips duplicates and flags any issues for review."

VISUAL: Go back to Contacts list, show tags filter]

NARRATOR:
"Now let's talk about segmentation using tags."

[ACTION: Click on tag filter, select "b2b"]

NARRATOR:
"By clicking on any tag, you instantly filter your contacts. Here I'm 
viewing only my B2B contacts - perfect for targeted outreach."

[ACTION: Navigate to Segments page]

NARRATOR:
"For more complex filtering, use Segments. Segments are saved searches that 
update dynamically."

[ACTION: Create new segment called "Hot Export Prospects"]

NARRATOR:
"Let me create a segment for hot export prospects. I'll set criteria: tag 
contains 'hot', industry equals export, created within last 30 days."

[ACTION: Show segment results]

NARRATOR:
"This gives me a target list of high-potential leads for my next campaign. 
Segments are incredibly powerful for focused selling."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[SECTION 3: SALES PIPELINE - 7:00 to 10:30]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

VISUAL: Navigate to Deals/Pipeline view, show Kanban board

NARRATOR:
"Chapter 3: The Sales Pipeline.

The pipeline view is where deals come to life. Let me show you around."

[ACTION: Show pipeline stages from left to right]

NARRATOR:
"Our pipeline has five stages, moving from left to right:

Stage 1: LEAD (blue) - Initial inquiry captured
Stage 2: QUALIFIED (yellow) - Needs verified, budget confirmed  
Stage 3: PROPOSAL (orange) - Quote sent to prospect
Stage 4: NEGOTIATION (red) - Active price/terms discussion
Stage 5: CLOSED (green or dark) - Won or Lost"

[ACTION: Hover over stage headers to show probabilities]

NARRATOR:
"Each stage has a default win probability. Lead starts at 10%, increasing 
as deals progress. Closed Won is 100%, Closed Lost is 0%."

[ACTION: Click "+ New Deal" in Lead column]

NARRATOR:
"Creating a new deal is straightforward. Let's add one now."

[ACTION: Fill in deal details - "TechAlgeria Software License"]

NARRATOR:
"I'll enter a deal name, link it to Ahmed Benali who we just added, set 
the value at 500,000 DZD, and assign it to myself."

[ACTION: Show probability slider, set expected close date]

NARRATOR:
"I can adjust the default probability if I have inside information. For 
this warm referral, I'll bump it up to 25%. And I expect to close by 
end of month."

[ACTION: Save deal, show it appearing in Lead column]

NARRATOR:
"The deal appears in the Lead column. Now watch this..."

[ACTION: Drag deal from Lead to Qualified]

NARRATOR:
"After qualifying Ahmed - confirming his budget, authority, need, and 
timeline - I simply drag the card to the Qualified stage."

[ACTION: Show activity note popup]

NARRATOR:
"The system prompts me to log why I moved the deal. This creates an audit 
trail and helps analyze patterns later."

[ACTION: Continue dragging through stages quickly]

NARRATOR:
"As the deal progresses, I continue dragging it through Proposal and 
Negotiation stages until finally..."

[ACTION: Drag to Closed Won, show celebration animation]

NARRATOR:
"...Closed Won! The system records the win date and credits it to my 
performance metrics."

VISUAL: Show Analytics → Forecasts page]

NARRATOR:
"All these deals feed into your forecast. The system calculates weighted 
pipeline value by multiplying each deal's value by its probability."

[ACTION: Point to forecast number]

NARRATOR:
"This forecast number helps leadership predict revenue and plan resources. 
It's updated in real-time as you work your pipeline."

[SECTION 4: ACTIVITIES & TASKS - 10:30 to 13:00]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

VISUAL: Open a contact record, scroll to Activity Timeline]

NARRATOR:
"Chapter 4: Activities and Tasks.

Every interaction with a contact should be logged. This builds a complete

[ACTION: Click "+ Log Activity", select "Call"]

NARRATOR:
"To log an activity, go to the contact's Activity Timeline and click

[ACTION: Fill in call details - duration, notes about discussion]

NARRATOR:
"I just got off the phone with Ahmed. I'll log the call, note that we 
discussed pricing, and record that he seemed interested but concerned 
about budget."

[ACTION: Check "Schedule Follow-up", set date 3 days out]

NARRATOR:
"Crucially, I always schedule a follow-up before finishing. The system 
will remind me in 3 days to reach out again with a proposal."

[ACTION: Save activity, show it appearing in timeline]

NARRATOR:
"The activity now appears in the timeline, visible to anyone on my team 
who views this contact."

VISUAL: Navigate to Tasks page]

NARRATOR:
"Tasks help you manage your daily workload. Let's look at the Tasks view."

[ACTION: Show task list with priorities]

NARRATOR:
"Tasks are color-coded by priority: Critical (red), High (orange), 
Medium (yellow), and Low (green)."

[ACTION: Create new task, assign to team member]

NARRATOR:
"I can create tasks for myself or delegate to team members. When assigning, 
add context so they know exactly what's needed."

[ACTION: Show task completion checkbox]

NARRATOR:
"When complete, check the box. Completed tasks move to your history for 
reference but don't clutter your active list."
