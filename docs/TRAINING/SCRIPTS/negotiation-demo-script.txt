[SECTION 4: BEST PRACTICES & COMMON MISTAKES - 9:30 to 11:15]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

VISUAL: Split screen with tips on left, mistakes on right]

NARRATOR:
"Section 4: Best Practices and Common Mistakes.

Let's cover strategies that lead to successful negotiations and pitfalls 
to avoid."

[ACTION: Highlight best practices list]

NARRATOR:
"BEST PRACTICES FOR BUYERS:

1. Research market prices before making an offer
   - Check competitor listings on AlgeriaTrade.dz
   - Understand typical discount ranges for your product category

2. Start reasonable - aim for 15-20% below asking price
   - Extreme lowballs get rejected or ignored
   - Shows you've done your homework

3. Always include a message with context
   - Explain your situation
   - Mention volume or repeat business potential
   - Be professional and courteous

4. Respond promptly - within 24 hours ideally
   - Shows you're serious and organized
   - Keeps momentum in the negotiation

5. Know your maximum price before starting
   - Set a walk-away point
   - Don't get caught up in the moment"

[ACTION: Highlight best practices for sellers]

NARRATOR:
"BEST PRACTICES FOR SELLERS:

1. Review buyer profile before responding
   - Check verification status and ratings
   - Consider order history

2. Use the margin calculator every time
   - Never guess at your profitability
   - Know your absolute floor price

3. Add value in your counter-offers
   - Warranty extensions
   - Free shipping or upgrades
   - Priority service

4. Consider lifetime value, not just this transaction
   - A slightly lower price may win a loyal customer
   - One deal can lead to many more

5. Set appropriate auto-accept thresholds
   - Saves time on small concessions
   - Typically 3-5% below asking price"

[ACTION: Highlight common mistakes with X marks]

NARRATOR:
"COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID:

❌ LOWBALLING EXCESSIVELY
Offering 50%+ off insults sellers and damages your reputation.
Start at 15-20% max reduction.

❌ IGNORING TIME PRESSURE
Letting offers expire looks unprofessional. Set reminders!
Respond within 24 hours even if just to acknowledge.

❌ BEING UNPROFESSIONAL
Aggressive language, threats, or rudeness burns bridges.
The B2B community remembers negative experiences.

❌ NOT KNOWING YOUR NUMBERS
Sellers who don't know their costs accept losing deals.
Buyers who don't know their budget overcommit.

❌ MAKING IT PERSONAL
This is business, not emotional. Stay objective and focused
on finding mutual value."
