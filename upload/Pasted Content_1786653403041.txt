# MASTER PROMPT — BUILD A COMPLETE ALGERIAN B2B MARKETPLACE

## 1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Build a complete, production-ready B2B marketplace inspired by the business model and functionality of Made-in-China.com, but designed specifically for the Algerian market.

The platform must connect:

* Algerian manufacturers
* Algerian distributors
* Wholesalers
* Importers
* Exporters
* Retailers
* SMEs
* Large enterprises
* Public-sector organizations
* International buyers looking for Algerian products

The platform must NOT be a simple e-commerce website.

It must be a complete B2B sourcing, supplier discovery, RFQ, quotation, communication, procurement and business networking platform.

Use Made-in-China.com as a functional benchmark, NOT as a source for copying its code, copyrighted content, branding, images, text or visual identity.

Create an original Algerian brand, UI and architecture.

Suggested working name:

"AlgeriaTrade"

Alternative names can be proposed by the AI.
