     1→# MASTER PROMPT — BUILD A COMPLETE ALGERIAN B2B MARKETPLACE
     2→
     3→## 1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE
     4→
     5→Build a complete, production-ready B2B marketplace inspired by the business model and functionality of Made-in-China.com, but designed specifically for the Algerian market.
     6→
     7→The platform must connect:
     8→
     9→* Algerian manufacturers
    10→* Algerian distributors
    11→* Wholesalers
    12→* Importers
    13→* Exporters
    14→* Retailers
    15→* SMEs
    16→* Large enterprises
    17→* Public-sector organizations
    18→* International buyers looking for Algerian products
    19→
    20→The platform must NOT be a simple e-commerce website.
    21→
    22→It must be a complete B2B sourcing, supplier discovery, RFQ, quotation, communication, procurement and business networking platform.
    23→
    24→Use Made-in-China.com as a functional benchmark, NOT as a source for copying its code, copyrighted content, branding, images, text or visual identity.
    25→
    26→Create an original Algerian brand, UI and architecture.
    27→
    28→Suggested working name:
    29→
    30→"AlgeriaTrade"
    31→
    32→Alternative names can be proposed by the AI.
